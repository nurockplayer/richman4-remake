# Governance audit — 2026-10-14

The owner requested an independent simplification of active governance, Astra-led
engineering, and resumption of the full native playtest-and-repair mission.
This decision supersedes conflicting historical process directives. It changes
engineering governance, not the game's fidelity target or acceptance evidence.

The independent read-only Astra audit found competing role assignments, duplicated
workflow rules, contradictory refactor guidance, and a recovery-only handoff that
prevented progress from available source. It recommended the changes below.

## Boundaries retained

- Private-use high-fidelity Richman4; 90–95% player-perceived fidelity is a target,
  not an invented measured score. All required screens/flows need coverage.
- Godot standalone desktop, Apple Silicon macOS first, future Windows/Linux
  portability; no browser/development-environment dependency in the delivered app.
- Ordinary title/new-game input, human/AI continuity, legal financial and
  inventory actions, real disk save/fresh-process load, complete match,
  settlement, restart and normal quit. Fixtures cannot replace player evidence.
- Same-edition original/remake comparisons and independent visual acceptance;
  functional, native/package and presentation verdicts stay distinct. #1 closure
  remains an owner/Steward decision after integrated evidence.
- Deterministic simulation/RNG, safe save handling, no silent owner-save damage.
  Unreleased development saves do not acquire permanent compatibility promises;
  explicit, safe migration/incompatibility remains allowed as in the former #1.
- Independent final-candidate review, applicable hosted checks, unresolved valid
  P1/P2 debt disposition, no force-push or protection/acceptance bypass.
- Private pinned Git/LFS asset authority, publication rights and secret handling,
  isolated playtest profiles, one coordinated FULL lane and the disk hard stop.
- No implicit Tachiko source-of-truth/runtime/cutover/Hot Reload/M5 authorization.
